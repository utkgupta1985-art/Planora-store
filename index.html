What is PLANORA?

PLANORA is an independent digital-template project focused on simple planners, study trackers, printable worksheets and tools for everyday organization.

We want planning to feel approachable: useful pages, uncluttered layouts and low prices that make it easy to try something new.

The collection is designed around students and anyone who enjoys organizing tasks, goals, reading, habits or revision.

Products are intended as flexible tools—not promises of perfect productivity. Use what helps, skip what doesn't, and adapt each sheet to your own routine.

PLANORA is being built from the ground up, with new templates and improvements planned as the store develops.
